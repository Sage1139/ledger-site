Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

LEDGER is a free website that teaches how the stock market works using virtual money. This page explains what we collect and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • If you don't sign in: nothing is sent to us. Your lesson progress, language and virtual portfolio are stored only in your own browser.
  • If you sign in: your name, email address and profile picture (if you use Google), plus your lesson progress, language choice and virtual-money portfolio, so they can follow you across devices.
  • AI tutor questions: the text you type, along with which page or lesson you're on, is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate an answer (Google Gemini, with Groq as a backup when Gemini is busy). We don't store your chats. Please don't type personal or financial details.

What we never collect

We never ask for or store bank account, card, UPI, PAN, Aadhaar or demat details, passwords to other services, or OTPs. No real money is ever traded on this site.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase: account sign-in and storing your saved progress.
  • Vercel: hosting this website.
  • Google (Gemini API): answering AI tutor questions. On the free tier, Google may use submitted prompts to improve its products.
  • Groq: a backup AI provider for AURORA when Gemini is busy.
  • Brevo: sending account emails such as password resets.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: counting page visits without cookies or personal data.
  • Google Sign-In: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Razorpay: only if you buy LEDGER Pro, to process the payment. Razorpay handles your card, UPI or bank details; we never see or store them.

Your choices

You can delete your saved data at any time from your account page, and the home page, simulators and short AURORA answers work without an account. Lessons need a free account.
