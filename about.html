About LEDGER

Last updated: 26 November 2026

LEDGER is a free place to learn the Indian stock market from zero, and to practise with real market data and virtual money before risking a single real rupee.

Why we built it

Many of us wanted to try stocks, SIPs or F&O but were scared of losing money, and with good reason: SEBI found that about 88% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY2025-26. Most "learning" online is either too hard or a sales pitch for a tip service. We wanted a calm, honest place to learn by doing.

What you can do here

  • Learn: 23 short, interactive lessons across four levels, from "what is a share" to options.
  • Practise: a stock time machine, a SIP and mutual fund time machine, and a live trading terminal with ₹10 lakh of virtual money.
  • Ask: AURORA, our AI tutor, answers in 13 languages including Tamil, Hindi, Hinglish and Tanglish.

What we are not

We are not a broker, a SEBI-registered investment adviser or a research analyst. We never give buy or sell tips, and no real money is ever traded on LEDGER. For personal advice, please speak to a SEBI-registered professional.

Who runs LEDGER

LEDGER is built and run by an independent founder in India. Write to us any time at our support email.
