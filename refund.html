Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Everything free on LEDGER stays free and needs no payment. This policy covers the optional paid plan, LEDGER Pro, once it is available.

1. Cancelling

You can cancel Pro at any time from your account page or by emailing our support email. Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for, and then your account returns to the free plan with all your progress intact.

2. Refunds

  • First payment: if you're not happy, ask within 7 days of your first Pro payment and we'll refund it in full, no questions asked.
  • Renewals: renewals are not refunded, but you can cancel any time to stop the next one.
  • Charged by mistake or twice: tell us and we'll refund the extra amount in full.

3. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or net banking) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

4. How to ask

Email our support email from your account's email address with the payment date, amount and payment ID.
